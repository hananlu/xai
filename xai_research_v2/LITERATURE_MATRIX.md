# Targeted literature overlap matrix

Not a systematic review; no exhaustive screening, citation-network saturation or independent reviewers. Reading status means full text was obtained and the listed relevant sections examined, not that every equation was independently verified. Paper results below are author-reported, not reproduced here.

## Subsequent full-text checks

[Lee et al., fault classification (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr14203341): known fault injections, separability gates, matched references and deletion faithfulness already support a network evidence audit. This is substantially closer than generic XAI surveys. Our candidate differs in forecasting future volume under observation loss, without ground-truth natural-outage demand. We must not claim to introduce intervention-based validation, reference controls or collection-condition auditing.

[Zhong et al., repair-based anomaly explanation (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16115450): full text obtained, 23 pages; introduction and evaluation/repair design inspected. Counterfactual variable repair and comparisons against random/heuristic repairs already exist. A future budgeted telemetry-recovery paper needs a different operational decision, explicit retrieval costs, and comparisons against active acquisition methods.

[Zhou and Xiong, sequential auditing (2 Nov 2026 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03928): full text obtained; task formulation and experiments inspected. Queries missing **outcome labels** to decide connected interval violations, rather than repairing historical inputs for future traffic forecasts. Query efficiency itself is prior art.

Additional title scouting, abstract/primary-record level only: [Dynamic Measurement Scheduling, ICML 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/chang19a.html), [Bounded-Abstention Multi-horizon Forecasting, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04714), [Selective Forecasting via Metalearning, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23448), and [Longitudinal Active Feature Acquisition, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07452). None is counted as a completed full-text comparison.
